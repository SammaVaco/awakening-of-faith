How the words were chosen

Three draws each contributed 125 word occurrences. Each draw took one word uniformly at random from each of twelve equal portions of each of the five readers. It also took one from every printed BDK page reference in the reader’s stated range with translated text. For that step, a commentary clause was eligible under the BDK page or range it explains. Pages 2, 4, and 10 have no translated words in these readers. The population included articles, bracketed additions, and words with no alignment.

A fourth draw selected ten further occurrences in each reader, one from each of ten equal portions after excluding common function words. This made it easier to detect errors in the vocabulary. The random seeds were 804109, 32790541, 9106283, and 4215739 respectively. There were 425 selections and 424 distinct occurrences.

I compared the selected words with the Chinese in their clauses. In the third draw, 105 of 125 agreed with the independent reading on first inspection. Ten of the other twenty showed only the clause. In the additional vocabulary draw, 30 of 50 agreed on first inspection, and eleven of the remaining twenty showed only the clause. I corrected the identified errors and rechecked all 425 selections in Chromium by opening a clause and moving the pointer onto the sampled English word. I also checked taps at four sampled positions per reader.

Coverage across all five readers

Of 73,030 English word occurrences, 34,383 have a word match and 6,732 have a phrase match. A further 341 have a tentative word or phrase match and 2 point to a documented variant. The reader identifies English grammar for 23,670 and translator-supplied wording for 1,732. For 6,170 occurrences, it displays the clause with no narrower match. The unreviewed alignments are provisional.
